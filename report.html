The 0% Borrow Engine & Options Mechanics (30% Weight)

Figure 2.0: Spout Trading & Borrowing Console — Real-time pricing across US Equities, ETFs, and Tech leaders.

1. Harvesting the Variance Risk Premium (VRP)

The mathematical linchpin of Spout’s 0% interest rate is the Variance Risk Premium (VRP): equity options systematically trade at an Implied Volatility (IV) that exceeds actual Realized Volatility (RV). Because option buyers consistently pay a surplus for catastrophe insurance, systematic option sellers extract positive risk-adjusted carry over time.

Spout automates covered call sales on the underlying collateral pool on regulated US exchanges, distributing 80% of option premium directly to stablecoin lenders while routing 20% to the protocol Insurance Fund.

Figure 2.1: The 0% Leverage Mechanism — Spout lends cash against collateral funded by programmatic options flow.

2. The Flat 50% LTV & Per-Asset Delta Tuning

Rather than complicating user experience with fractional LTVs, Spout adopts a flat 50% LTV across all collateral assets. To absorb varying asset risk (e.g. low-beta gold ETF GLD vs. high-beta AI stock NVDA), Spout tunes the covered call strike distance (Delta Δ) per asset:

  • GLD (Gold): ~0.15 Beta → 0.10 Δ (Far Out-of-the-Money) → ~4% liquidation buffer.
  • AAPL / GOOG: ~1.05 Beta → 0.15 Δ (Standard OTM) → ~6.5% liquidation buffer.
  • NVDA / TSLA: ~2.10 Beta → 0.20 Δ (Wide Volatility Buffer OTM) → ~12.5% liquidation buffer.

3. The 3-Tier Loss Waterfall

To insulate institutional stablecoin deposits from tail-risk crashes, Spout enforces an immutable 3-tier loss waterfall:

  1. Layer 1 — Insurance Fund (First-Loss): Seeded at launch ($50k–$100k) and continuously fortified by a 20% protocol fee cut until reaching 2% pool AUM.
  2. Layer 2 — Junior Tranche (Second-Loss): Subordinated risk capital capturing boosted 15–25% APY in exchange for absorbing secondary deficits.
  3. Layer 3 — Senior Tranche (Protected Capital): Priority 7% yield allocation protected by the preceding layers. Across historical backtests, zero losses have ever reached Senior capital.
Figure 2.3: Lending Tranche Structure — Senior (steady priority yield) vs. Junior (elevated yield risk buffer).

4. The ITM Assignment Paradox & Auto-Roll Reconstitution

When an equity rallies past the strike, the covered call exercises. The borrower is NOT liquidated. The cash proceeds first automatically extinguish the borrower's debt. With Auto-Roll ON (default), residual cash instantly repurchases the equity at market price and enters the next cycle. The borrower retains 100% of their principal, with average historical assignment friction averaging only ~0.5% annualized.

High-Leverage Product Feature Recommendations (Product Insight)

1. Synthetic Delta-Hedged Collars ("100% Downside Protection")

Allow borrowers to opt into a Protective Collar Vault. A fraction of the covered call premium (+3% of +12% APY) is systematically used to purchase Out-Of-The-Money put options matching the liquidation threshold. Borrowers receive slightly lower net yield share but gain absolute downside liquidation immunity during market crashes.

2. Cross-Margin Collateral Baskets

Permit users to deposit diversified portfolios (e.g., 40% NVDA + 40% GOOG + 20% GLD). Because cross-asset covariance dampens total portfolio volatility (σp), Spout can safely boost borrowing LTV from 50% to 65% on diversified baskets with identical risk parameters.

3. 1-Click Flash Deleveraging (Self-Repay)

Provide a 1-click button allowing borrowers approaching liquidation to atomically sell just enough locked equity collateral to eliminate debt without needing external stablecoins or paying partial liquidation penalties.
