1. Solana Token-2022 Transfer Hooks vs. Broken ERC Wrappers

Unlike legacy Ethereum permissioned standards (ERC-3643 / ERC-1400) which frequently break decentralized exchange composability, Spout implements native Solana Token-2022 Transfer Hooks (spl-transfer-hook).

  • On-Chain Enforcement: Every balance transfer of spNVDA, spAAPL, or spGLD programmatically executes an extra instruction validating KYC-compliance in Spout's registry PDA.
  • Zero-PII Exposure: Sensitive identity records stored off-chain with licensed identity providers. Only an immutable 1-bit verification flag resides on-chain.
  • Universal DeFi Composability: The token retains the standard SPL interface, readily composable across Solana money markets and AMMs without bespoke wrappers.

Alpaca Securities LLC (FINRA-registered, SIPC-member US broker-dealer) holds segregated customer equities covered up to $500,000, while Spout Finance Inc. operates as a registered FinCEN MSB governing on-chain credit facilities.

The 0% Borrow Engine & Options Mechanics

Figure 2.0 — Spout Trading & Borrowing Console: Real-time pricing across US Equities, ETFs, and Tech leaders.

1. Harvesting the Variance Risk Premium (VRP)

The mathematical linchpin of Spout’s 0% interest rate is the Variance Risk Premium (VRP): equity options systematically trade at an Implied Volatility (IV) exceeding actual Realized Volatility (RV). Systematic option sellers extract positive risk-adjusted carry over time.

Spout automates covered call sales on regulated US exchanges, distributing 80% of option premium directly to stablecoin lenders while routing 20% to the protocol Insurance Fund.

2. The Flat 50% LTV & Per-Asset Delta Tuning

Spout adopts a flat 50% LTV across all collateral assets, tuning the covered call strike distance (Delta Δ) per asset:

  • GLD (Gold): ~0.15 Beta → 0.10 Δ (Far OTM) → ~4% liquidation buffer.
  • AAPL / GOOG: ~1.05 Beta → 0.15 Δ (Standard OTM) → ~6.5% liquidation buffer.
  • NVDA / TSLA: ~2.10 Beta → 0.20 Δ (Wide Volatility Buffer OTM) → ~12.5% liquidation buffer.

4. The ITM Assignment Paradox & Auto-Roll Reconstitution

When an equity rallies past the strike, the covered call exercises. The borrower is NOT liquidated. Cash proceeds automatically extinguish the borrower’s debt. With Auto-Roll ON (default), residual cash instantly repurchases equity at market price — average historical friction averaging only ~0.5% annualized.

🚨 Finding 1: 24/7 DeFi vs. TradFi Market Hours Dissonance
High Severity

Solana blocks settle 24/7, while the NYSE and CBOE close on weekends and national holidays. A black-swan event during a Saturday crypto selloff creates severe pricing divergence when underlying shares cannot be traded at Alpaca.

Recommendation: Institute an On-Chain Weekend Buffer Protocol that suspends hard liquidations during exchange holidays unless verified multi-venue oracle circuit breakers trigger.

Medium Severity

Part IV · Product Insight

High-Leverage Product Feature Recommendations

1. Synthetic Delta-Hedged Collars (“100% Downside Protection”)

Allow borrowers to opt into a Protective Collar Vault. A fraction of the covered call premium (+3% of +12% APY) is systematically used to purchase OTM put options matching the liquidation threshold. Borrowers receive slightly lower net yield but gain absolute downside liquidation immunity during market crashes.

2. Cross-Margin Collateral Baskets

Permit users to deposit diversified portfolios (e.g., 40% NVDA + 40% GOOG + 20% GLD). Because cross-asset covariance dampens total portfolio volatility (σp), Spout can safely boost borrowing LTV from 50% to 65% on diversified baskets with identical risk parameters.

3. 1-Click Flash Deleveraging (Self-Repay)

Provide a 1-click button allowing borrowers approaching liquidation to atomically sell just enough locked equity collateral to eliminate debt without needing external stablecoins or paying partial liquidation penalties.
